How it works

Three steps from an empty folder to a finished plan.

1

Pick a set or a Pokémon

Templates for master sets, Pokédex binders and “every card of one Pokémon” – or search card by card. Filter by era, set, card kind, rarity and type.

2

Arrange the slots

Drag, sort, leave pages free. Grids from 2×2 to 5×5, Reverse Holo, 1st Edition and special prints as their own slots.

3

Print & tick off

Placeholders at 63 × 88 mm, 9 per A4 sheet, each labelled “page · slot”. Bought the card? Tick it – the buy list only shows what is still missing.

Frequently asked questions

May I print the placeholders?

Yes – for private use as placeholders in your own binder. The prints are black and white, carry a watermark and must not be sold, traded or passed off as real cards. Binderplan is an unofficial fan tool with no affiliation to The Pokémon Company or Nintendo.

Where do the prices come from?

From the Cardmarket trend (euros) via the TCGdex database. They are a guide, not an offer – some old cards have no Cardmarket entry, then you will see a “?”.

Can I use German card names and images?

Yes. The card language (DE/EN) is switched in the filter column and applies to names, images, set names and the PDF. For the old WotC sets (Base Set, Jungle, Fossil …) there are no German scans in the database; those cards are marked “EN”.

What are credits?

Credits are the unit for the AI artwork pages – only for those, everything else is included in the plan. An artwork page costs 10 credits; with more than two cards on the page, 2 more per card (30 at most). You see the cost of a page before you start. If a generation fails technically, the credits come back automatically. Credits bought on their own never expire.

How do I cancel?

With two clicks at binderplan.app/kuendigen – no login required. Your access stays until the end of the paid term, after that your account continues for free. Purchased credits are yours to keep.

Do I need an account?

Not for planning. You need a free account for the card PDF export and to see your binders on several devices.

Why are the legal pages in German?

Binderplan is run from Germany. Legal notice, terms, withdrawal policy and privacy policy are therefore provided in German, as required by German law. The app itself and this page are fully available in English.
